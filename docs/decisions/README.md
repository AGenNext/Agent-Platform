# Decision Records

This directory holds the decision records that `AGENTS.md` requires before a
design change (§Design Change Control) or a language/vocabulary/ontology change
(§Language, Vocabulary, and Ontology Change Control) is implemented.

> **Status of the records here is `PROPOSED`, not `APPROVED`.** They are drafted
> to document the changes in the associated pull request so quorum can review
> them. Per `AGENTS.md`, *no quorum, no design change* and *no quorum, no
> vocabulary change* — these records do not themselves grant approval. Approver
> slots are left **PENDING**; they must be filled by the actual quorum reviewers,
> not by the author or any agent. Once approved, a record becomes an append-only
> canonical record and must not be edited or deleted.
